Common questions

Don't see yours? Call or text 512-922-7602.

Which states are you licensed in?

I'm based in Austin and serve the greater Austin area. I'm also licensed in Minnesota, Wisconsin, Florida and California, which helps if you're relocating between any of them.

I'm self-employed. Can I still qualify?

Yes. Business owners buy homes all the time. It takes the right documentation and the right program, and I'll tell you exactly what's needed for your situation.

How much do I need for a down payment?

Usually less than people expect. Depending on the program it can be 0% (VA), 3.5% (FHA) or 3% (some conventional loans). We'll also check for down payment assistance you might qualify for.

Will getting pre-approved hurt my credit?

A pre-approval includes a credit inquiry, but multiple mortgage inquiries within a short shopping window are generally treated as one. Our first conversation doesn't need a credit pull at all.

What documents will I need?

Usually recent pay stubs, W-2s or tax returns, bank statements and a photo ID. I'll send you a checklist for your situation so you only gather what's actually needed.

How long does it take to close?

Many purchase loans close in about 30 days from an accepted offer. It depends on the program, the appraisal and how quickly documents come in. Being pre-approved up front helps a lot.

Is now a good time to refinance?

It depends on your current rate, how long you plan to stay and what you're trying to accomplish. I'll run a break-even analysis so you can see whether it saves you money.
