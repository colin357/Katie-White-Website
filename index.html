FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Which states are you licensed in?

I'm based in Austin and serve the greater Austin area, and I'm also licensed to lend in Minnesota, Wisconsin, Florida and California. Relocating between any of them? Even better.

I'm self-employed. Can I still qualify?

Yes. Entrepreneurs and small business owners buy homes every day — it just takes the right documentation and the right program. I'll walk you through exactly what's needed for your situation.

How much do I need for a down payment?

Less than most people think. Depending on the program, it can be 0% (VA, USDA), 3.5% (FHA) or 3% (some conventional loans). We'll also look at down payment assistance programs you may qualify for.

Will getting pre-approved hurt my credit?

A pre-approval involves a credit inquiry, but multiple mortgage inquiries within a short shopping window are generally treated as one. Our first conversation doesn't require a credit pull at all.

What documents will I need?

Typically recent pay stubs, W-2s or tax returns, bank statements and a photo ID. I'll send a personalized checklist so you only gather what you actually need.

How long does it take to close?

Many purchase loans close in roughly 30 days from an accepted offer, depending on the loan program, appraisal and how quickly documents come in. Getting pre-approved up front keeps everything on track.

Is now a good time to refinance?

It depends on your current rate, how long you plan to stay and your goals. I'll run a break-even analysis so you can see whether it actually saves you money.
